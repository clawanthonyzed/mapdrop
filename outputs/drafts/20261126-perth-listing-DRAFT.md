## Description
A clean, minimalist street map of Perth, Western Australia, with the Swan River, Kings Park and the CBD street grid drawn in soft warm tones. The city name and coordinates sit at the bottom of the print.

What you get
- Digital download: one high resolution PNG file, 3600 x 4800 pixels (12 x 16 inches at 300 dpi). Prints sharp at A3 and smaller. Print at home, at Officeworks or at any local print shop.
- Printed poster option (if enabled): printed on matte poster paper and posted to you by our print partner. Frame not included.

Good for
- A housewarming gift for someone who just moved to Perth
- A keepsake for a Perth local who now lives away
- A clean, calm piece of wall art for a home office, hallway or living room
