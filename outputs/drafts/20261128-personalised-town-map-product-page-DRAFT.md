## Page title
Your Town, Your Map: Personalised Street Map Poster of Any Australian Town, Suburb or Address

## Short line under the title
The streets, coastline and rivers of the place you love, drawn as clean wall art. Printable at home or at any print shop.

## How it works
1. **Type a place.** A town, a suburb or a street address. The map is centred on it.
2. **Pick a style.** Clean, Night, Blueprint or Terracotta.
3. **Pick a size.** A4 and A3 files come in every order. A2 prints well from the A3 file.
4. **Add your words.** The big name line and the small second line are yours, for example "FREMANTLE" and "Where we met, 2019".
5. **Pay and download.** Ready towns download at once. Custom places are drawn after payment and the link arrives by email, usually within 30 minutes and always within 24 hours.

## What you get
- A high resolution PNG at 300 dots per inch for A3 (3537 x 4992 pixels) and A4.
- A print-ready PDF.
- Map area: about 2.5 km around your point for a town centre, or about 750 m for one street.
- Coastlines, rivers, parks and every street from OpenStreetMap, the open map built by volunteers.

## Good for
- A housewarming gift for a first home
- A wedding or anniversary map of where it happened
- A keepsake of a home town for someone who moved away
- A calm, simple piece of wall art for a hallway, office or holiday house

## Printing tips
- Print on matte poster paper or 200 gsm card at Officeworks or any local print shop.
- Choose "fit to page" for A4 and A3. For A2, ask the shop to print the A3 file at A2.
- Colours can look slightly different on screen and on paper.

## Questions
**Can you map a small town or a rural address?** Yes, if OpenStreetMap has the streets. If your place has very few mapped streets, we email you a preview before drawing the final file and refund you if you do not like it.
**Can I change the words after buying?** Yes, once, within 7 days. Reply to the download email.
**Do you post printed posters?** Not yet. Digital files only for now.
**Can I sell the file?** No. Files are for personal use and gifts only.
