# MapDrop — SEO Context

## Venture
MapDrop sells city map poster PDFs as instant downloads on Etsy and Gumroad. Also sells physical prints via Printify. Maps generated from OpenStreetMap data.

## Target Buyer
- Moving to a new city / nostalgic about hometown
- Buying a housewarming or travel gift
- Decorating home office or living room
- Design-conscious, 25-45, mostly female on Etsy

## Platform
- Primary: Etsy (digital downloads + physical print)
- Secondary: Gumroad (bundles), LemonSqueezy (subscription)

## Core Keywords (high-volume Etsy)
- city map print
- city map poster
- minimalist city map
- [city name] map
- [city name] poster
- [city name] wall art
- travel poster
- city art print
- urban map print
- instant download map
- printable city map
- housewarming gift map
- custom city print

## Etsy Title Formula
`[City] Map Print | [Style] City Poster | [Use Case] | Instant Download`

## Tag Rules (13 tags max, Etsy)
- 2 tags: city-specific (e.g., "sydney map", "sydney poster")
- 3 tags: style/format (minimalist map, city print, urban poster)
- 3 tags: use case (housewarming gift, travel poster, office decor)
- 3 tags: format (instant download, digital print, printable art)
- 2 tags: broad (wall art, home decor)

## Competitor Signals
- "MapPress", "CityPrintStudio", "UrbanMapArt" — all top Etsy sellers
- Bestselling style: minimal (white background, black roads)
- Bestselling size listings: A3, A2, 24×36 inch
